# Triage Buddy — demo script (~2 minutes)

Narration is casual, in the speaker's own words. The lines below are a
guide, not a teleprompter — say it like you're showing a teammate.

---

**[0:00 — the problem]**

“Okay so, triage. Every morning someone on the team opens Jira and there
are twenty new issues, and somebody has to read each one, guess how bad it
is, guess who should own it, and write the first ‘hey, we saw this’ reply.
That is the whole job I'm trying to shrink.”

**[0:20 — the panel]**

“Here's a real one: ‘App crashes on login.’ I open the Triage Buddy panel
on the issue. It says Highest priority — because, yeah, crash on login —
labels crash and auth, and it thinks Ana should own it, because she fixed
the two closest matches before. It even shows those two issues, so you can
check its work. And there's a draft first reply ready to go.”

**[0:50 — apply it]**

“I hit Apply this triage. Priority, labels, assignee — done, one click.
The issue is now Highest, tagged, assigned to Ana.”

**[1:05 — the Rovo agent]**

“Same brain lives in Rovo Chat too. I ask it: ‘who should own this
issue?’ It answers Ana, and tells me why — same two issues. Then: ‘draft
a first reply for this issue.’ It writes one you can send as-is. And the
important bit — it never changes anything on its own. It only applies a
suggestion when you tell it to.”

**[1:35 — the honesty bit]**

“One more thing I like: when there's no evidence, it says so. New project,
no history? It tells you nobody obvious owns this yet instead of picking
a random name. That's the whole idea — triage you can trust because it
shows its work.”

**[1:50 — close]**

“Triage Buddy. Rovo agent plus a panel, one click to apply. That's it.”

## Shots to capture (for the video)

1. Jira issue open: “App crashes on login” (or any real crash issue).
2. Triage Buddy panel: suggestion visible (Highest, labels, Ana, draft reply, similar issues).
3. Click “Apply this triage”, show the issue updated.
4. Rovo Chat: ask “who should own this issue?” then “draft a first reply”.
5. A second issue with no history (e.g. a typo) to show the Low priority
   and the “nobody obvious yet” owner line.
